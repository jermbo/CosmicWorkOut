# Logging date

You forgot to log yesterday's run, or you logged the wrong weight last week. This page tells how you fix any past day: with one date that every tracker shares.

## One date for the whole app

The app holds one logging date. The Overview, Habits, Activity log, Health, Baselines, and today's routine all read it, and each new record gets it. It starts as today. When you reload the app, it is today again, so you never log today's water on last Tuesday by accident.

You change the date in two ways. Each tracker screen and the Overview have the same header. You tap its date and pick a day, or you tap a past day in its week strip. A future day cannot be selected.

## How to fix a past day

1. Pick the day in the header.
2. Open the tracker, or stay in it.
3. Log, edit, or delete as you do for today.

A session is the one case with a question. When the logging date is not today and you tap Start, the app asks a question such as "Log workout for Mon, Nov 2?". A session takes time, so a start on the wrong day is costly. A habit tap is cheap to undo, so it gets no question. When a session is already logged for that date, the routine screen shows Edit and Delete for it.

## No lock on old days

Any past day is open, in each tracker, the same as today. A lock would only stop you from fixing a real mistake, and there is one user, so no one else's data is at risk.

## Why there is no history screen

A separate calendar of history did two jobs: you saw the past, and you changed it. The two jobs are now in better places. Insights shows the past — gaps and streaks show in the heat chart and the "Showing Up" chart. The logging date changes the past, in the screen that already knows how to edit that record. So the calendar went away.

### Example

On Wednesday you see that Monday has no run. You tap Monday in the week strip. You open the Activity log card, add "Run, 30 min, Moderate", and it saves with Monday's date. You reload, and the app is back on Wednesday.
