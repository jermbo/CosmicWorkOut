# Activity log

Not every workout follows a plan. You played pickleball, you walked the dog, you went to a yoga class. The Activity log keeps a short record of each, with no plan and no session. It is off until you turn it on in Settings.

## One entry, three choices

You open the Activity log for the logging date and tap to add. A sheet asks three things:

1. **Type.** A type from your own list, or Other. With Other, you type a name, and Save stays off until it has one.
2. **Duration.** It starts at 30 minutes. Plus and minus move it by 5, and you can hold them. It stays between 1 and 300 minutes.
3. **Intensity.** Easy, Moderate, or Hard.

You save, the entry shows in the list for that date, and the [Mascot](mascot.md) comes by to celebrate. The next time that you add an entry, the sheet starts on the type that you used last.

## Your own list of types

The list is yours. A new list holds Stretching and Cardio. Other is not a type: it is the way to add one. Save Other with a new name, and that name joins the list. Save Other with a name that is in the list, in any mix of capital letters, and the entry uses that type; nothing is added. The picker shows the type that you use most first, then the rest by name, and Other last.

In Settings › Activity you can rename a type, and every past entry follows; a rename to a name that exists joins the two types. You can hide a type: it leaves the picker and Activity Mix, but its entries stay. Type its name with Other, or turn it back on, and it returns.

## Edit and delete

You tap an entry to change it. Delete asks for a second tap, so a slip of the thumb does not remove it. To fix a past day, pick the date in the header, as the [logging date](logging-date.md) page tells.

## Why an activity is not a plan

A run has no sets, no routine, and no next step. If it were a plan, you would have to make a plan of one routine to log one run. So the app keeps two kinds apart: a structured practice that guides you, and a quick record of a thing that you did. A new sport is one more entry in your list of types — you add it by typing it once, with no new design.

## Where activities show

The Activity log card on the Overview lists the entries of the date. The week strip shows a mark on each day with an activity. Insights has an Activity Mix chart, with one slice for each visible type, named after it.

### Example

On Saturday you mow the grass for an hour. You open the Activity log, tap to add, select Other, type "Mowing", press plus six times to reach 60 minutes, and save. Mowing is now a type. Next Saturday it is in the picker, and Activity Mix shows Mowing as its own slice.
