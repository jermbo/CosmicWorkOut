# Baselines

A baseline is a daily floor that you set so low that you can always reach it: ten pushups, a ten-minute walk, one page. The point is to show up each day, and to see growth over months. It is off until you turn it on in Settings.

## What a baseline has

You make a baseline in Settings → Baselines. It has a name and one or more metrics. Each metric is a duration, a distance (miles, kilometres, metres, or yards), or a count with a word that you type, such as "reps" or "words". Each metric has its floor. A baseline called "Daily 10" can hold Pushups as a count of 10 and Walk as a duration of 10 minutes.

A metric's kind is fixed when you make it, because the logs of the past depend on it. You can remove a metric; it then hides, and its logs stay.

## Many entries add up to a day

You log on the Baselines screen for the logging date, as often as you like. Each entry holds the metrics that you filled in. The total of a metric for a day is the sum of its entries. A metric that has no value in any entry of the day shows "not logged", not zero, because an empty field is not a zero.

## Logging is what counts

Any entry marks the baseline done for that day. The numbers never fail a day. If a low day still counts, you keep the streak alive on a bad day, and that is the goal of a baseline.

## A neutral comparison

For each metric, the app shows the day's total minus the floor, with its sign: +20, or −2 min. For a walk more is better, for screen time less is better, so the app does not call a number good or bad.

## Where baselines show

Each baseline on the Baselines screen has its own progress chart. The Overview card shows which baselines are done for the date. Insights has a Baseline Growth chart, which marks your best day with a ring, and a Time of Day chart, which shows when you log.

### Example

"Daily 10" has Pushups (10) and Walk (10 min). In the morning you log 12 pushups. At night you log 8 pushups and a 25-minute walk. The day is done. Pushups shows a total of 20, +10; Walk shows 25 min, +15 min.
