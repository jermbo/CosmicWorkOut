# Built-in templates

When you make a plan, you can start from a template instead of from scratch. This page is the contract for the templates that ship with the app.

## The templates

| Template         | Day A             | Day B                    | Day C             | What it teaches                                 |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ------------------------ | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Beginner 101     | Machine Full Body | Bodyweight & Cables      | Isolation Focus   | Form on machines, pulldowns, and cables         |
| Beginner 102     | DB Push & Pull    | Unilateral Legs          | Full Body DB      | Dumbbell presses, rows, curls, and one-leg work |
| Beginner 103     | Barbell Basics    | Hinge & Incline          | Pull & Press      | The bar: squat, bench, row, and deadlift        |
| Intermediate 101 | Heavy Push        | Volume Push              | Incline & Delts   | Chest, shoulders, and triceps                   |
| Intermediate 102 | Heavy Pull        | Row Variations           | Hinge & Lats      | Deadlifts, rows, pull-ups, and arms             |
| Intermediate 103 | Squat & Hinge     | Unilateral & Front Squat | Posterior & Power | Legs and the posterior chain                    |

## Rules

- Every template has 4 weeks and 3 days per week: 12 sessions in all.
- Each template id starts with `st-`, such as `st-beginner-101`.
- A template is read-only. When you start from one, the app makes a copy, and you edit the copy.
- The app writes the templates again on each start. So a fix to a template reaches every device with the next update, and a plan that you made from it does not change.
