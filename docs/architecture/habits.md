# Habits

Habits are the small things that you want to do each day: water, reading, a few minutes of quiet. The Habits screen is a daily check-in for the logging date. It is off until you turn it on in Settings.

## The check-in

Each habit is a tile with a ring that fills toward its daily goal. You tap or hold plus and minus, or tap the number to type an exact value. A toggle habit is one tap. When a habit reaches its goal, the tile jolts and flashes.

The step fits the habit, so you do not tap 500 times for 500 words. A minutes habit moves by 5. A count moves by about a tenth of its goal, as a simple number: 4 cups moves by 1, and 500 words by 50.

## The five types

| Type    | You log               | Ring |
| ------- | --------------------- | ---- |
| Times   | How many times        | Yes  |
| Minutes | How long              | Yes  |
| Count   | A number, with a unit | Yes  |
| Boolean | Done or not done      | No   |
| Mood    | A value from −5 to +5 | No   |

## Built-in habits

The app adds five habits on the first start: Water (4 cups), Coffee (2 cups), Meditation (20 minutes), Writing (500 words), and Reading (30 minutes). It adds them only once, because after that they are yours to rename, reorder, set a new goal, or turn off.

## Mood

Mood is a strip of buttons across the screen, from −5 to +5, always in view. It is a protected habit: you cannot turn it off or delete it inside Habits, because the mood charts in Insights need it. But it hides with the Habits toggle, like the other habits. Mood has two colours, one for good days and one for bad days.

## Your own habits

In Settings → Habits you make, edit, reorder, and turn off habits, and you set the colour of each one. A habit that is off hides from the check-in, and its logs stay. A new habit gets the first colour of the palette that no other habit uses, so the charts stay easy to read.

## One value for each day

A habit has one value for each date. Each tap overwrites that value, so there is no list of taps to add up. To fix a past day, you pick the date in the header, as the [logging date](logging-date.md) page tells.

### Example

Your Water goal is 4 cups. You tap plus three times in the morning, and the ring is three-quarters full. In the evening you tap once more. The ring closes, the tile flashes, and the Overview card shows one more habit done.
