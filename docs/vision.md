# Vision

CosmicWorkOut is the simplest tool to follow a structured training plan on your phone. It works offline, and nothing gets between you and the record of what you did.

## The problem it solves

You finish a set, and you have a few seconds before the next one. A notes app is fast, but it does not know your plan or your last weight. A gym platform knows everything, but it needs an account, a connection, and many taps. So most people stop logging after two weeks.

This app sits between the two. It knows a multi-week plan and the weight you lifted last time. It logs a set with one tap. The test for every screen is one sentence: you log what you did in less than five seconds, with one hand, without thinking.

## Who it is for

It is for the developer and a small group of friends. They have a lifting plan, or they want one. They also want a quick place to log a run, a glass of water, or a bad day. They prefer a fast tool to a large one, and they do not always have a signal where they train.

## What success looks like

You finish a workout, and the session is in the app with no extra work. Three months later, you look back and see what you did. You never lose a record because the phone was offline. You do not think about the app; it simply works.

## What it is not

It is a personal notebook, not a fitness product. It has no social feed, no followers, and no leaderboard. It gives no coaching and no form feedback. It has no market for plans, no nutrition tracking, and no link to a wearable. These are not future features. They are outside what the product is.

Each new movement type is configuration, not a rewrite. A new guided practice becomes a new Discipline; a new sport becomes one more activity type. The [glossary](glossary.md) gives the test that decides which.
